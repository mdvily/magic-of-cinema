**For a while, **I had believed Cinema was dead. I had felt that ever since COVID-19 hit in 2020, a permanent stain on both the box office and movie quality was permanently gone. I was used to such masterpieces, and not this CGI fodder with bad writing. Even after the pandemic, 2021-2024 was an awful time for movies. But 2025 was next and it was so bad, I almost gave up on cinema. I saw 150 movies, 30 of them were films I did not dislike. But then came 2026. The year is bringing the box office back to its roots. And as for quality? The weaker films like Ice Cream Man and the Moana remake were not bad. But we had films like The Odyssey, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, The Furious, Obsession, Project Hail Mary, Michael, Toy Story 5, and so much more! But now that the final four months are here, I find it time to rank the 10 best to come. There is no accurate list, it is just my personal ranking, and only 10 films are on this list.
