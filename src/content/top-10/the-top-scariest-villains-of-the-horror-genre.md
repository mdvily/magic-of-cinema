  - title: Psycho
    year: 1960

    description: |-
      #10: Norman Bates: Psycho (1960-1990)
      SPOILERS
      We all knew this guy was bound to arrive on this list. Norman Bates is the definition of creepy and the scariest horror character of his time. His first kill: Stabbing Marion Crane in the iconic shower sequence. We see a woman screaming as loud as she can, as the knife hardly hits her body and blood comes to the shower floor. But a gruesome kill is not enough. Bates must have other features that set him apart. He does. You see, Mr. Bates suffers from Dissociative Identity Disorder, which means that he does horrible things such as the murders of Marion and Arbogast, because he believes it was his mother who did this. A long time ago, he poisoned his mother Norma and her boyfriend Chet with strychnines, and thinks his mother brainwashed him into killing Marion, Arbogast and nearly Lila. But another reason is how deceptive he is. Bates Motel does not seem very suspicious, and Norman feels like a normal human with his mother abusing him. And on top of that, after his kills, he just sinks her car and cleans up the bathroom, to hide the evidence. That is creepy. Yes, he is a human, but that makes him feel real.
  - title: Scream
    year: 1996
    posterUrl: 'https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/lr9ZIrmuwVmZhpZuTCW8D9g0ZJe.jpg'
    description: |

      #9: Ghostface: Scream (1996-)
      Now, I know what you might be thinking? How is Ghostface on this list? He is just a regular human being, and at the same time, if he gives you the phone call, just run away or get a knife. The second one detracts how scary he is, but why am I saying “he”? Ghostface is not always a male. It can be a female or multiple people. What makes Ghostface scary is that you never know who is behind his mask. It could be a family member, a friend, a friend’s family member, anyone. That is what makes Ghostface so creepy. You never who it is, so the only way to survive is to not trust anybody. And as for the phone call? You do not always get one. Sometimes he just comes into the house without warning you, and murdering his victim. Yes, if you follow the rules, like never saying you will be right back, you would survive the movie, but what about real life? Ghostface is the most equivalent of an actual serial killer. You might survive, but you would have to be very careful, and I would not blame you if you panic under this pressure.

      #8: The Xenomorph: Alien (1979)
      SPOILERS
      Now, I only include the Xenomorph from the original Alien, because I do not see the sequels as horror. But even at that, the Xenomorph is terrifying. It is a black alien that feeds on the crew members with an intent to kill them all. It is horrifying because you never know where it could be, just that you are stuck with it! I mean, it feeds its way through Kane’s stomach as a baby, and there seems to be no way to defeat it. Even as Ridley escapes with Jones and orders MOTHER to blow up the ship with everyone dead, the Xenomorph finds her. It has two layers of jaws, and can release the second one, with a higher chance of killing you. If you actually defeat it, you are a legend.
  - title: 'Winnie-the-Pooh: Blood and Honey 2'
    year: 2024
    posterUrl: 'https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/2sADrLwMQof6yYmrJRSa04tFZuS.jpg'

      #7: Winnie The Pooh/William Robin: Winnie The Pooh: Blood And Honey II (2024)
      SPOILERS
      I must confess: I do not actually like the Blood And Honey franchise. However, I find the second one to be a massive improvement over the first in every way. And how scary Pooh is, certainly is one of them. His goal is to kill all of Christopher Robin’s friends and family. But what gives him an edge over Christopher is that he was Christopher’s brother the entire time. Not only is that a unique plot twist, but he knows all of Chris’s family, and killing him is a hard task. But even if it works, it would not for long. Pooh and his team of Piglet, Owl and Tigger would just resurrect because of a supernatural healing factor mutated by a secret honey formula. This means that it would be very difficult to kill them, and even if you do, they would come right back. Even running for your life would not save you, as Pooh is extremely fast.
  - title: Friday the 13th
    year: 1980
    posterUrl: 'https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/uGGpnWHOmWTARVN9wbC1nPxNgps.jpg'

      #6: Jason Voorhees Friday The 13th (1980-)
      Remember how Michael Myers was extremely close to being in the top list? That is because he is brutal, very hard to kill, and has the highest kill count. But he does not make it, because he is rather slow, so if you run, you could survive. That is not the case with Jason Voorhees. Voorhees is basically Michael Myers, but even more indestructible. Unlike Myers who dies in Halloween Ends (2022), Voorhees is now immortal. And unlike him, outrunning is not an option. Run as fast as you want, Voorhees will catch you. He is fast, near indestructible, and knows where you are hiding. Especially when he is angry.
  - title: The Exorcist
    year: 1973

      #5: Pazuzu: The Exorcist (1973)
      SPOILERS
      It honestly feels like a crime not to place the main antagonist from the “scariest movie ever” even higher on this list. But that just means there are some that could give you nightmares for months. Pazuzu is not an actual physical body. It is just a demon brainwashes you. But it does not just brainwash you. As it did with Regan MacNeil , it possesses her, and turns her into a completely different person. She acts cruel, throws up and cannot stay normal for the life of her. Even calling a pastor made things difficult. It took weeks for them to get Pazuzu to get out of her, and even then, she was never the same ever again. This means it never needed to kill Regan, just ruin her life forever. That is what makes this monster so scary.
  - title: The Conjuring 2
    year: 2016
    posterUrl: 'https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/zEqyD0SBt6HL7W9JQoWwtd5Do1T.jpg'
    description: |-
      #4: Valak: The Conjuring 2 (2016), The Nun (2018) and The Nun II (2023)
      SPOILERS
      Valak is arguably the most underrated of all of the horror villains, so it had to be on this list. I mean, if its actress Bonnie Aaron’s was also scared of her character, that means it is a real threat. Yes, Lorraine caught onto its name, but that does not matter when it did an excellent job at hiding the name. That makes Valak almost indestructible. It does not chase you. It gets in your head, and brainwashes you into committing suicide. It’s jumpscares are also are quiet and let the music talk, such as when the Warrens find answers from Bill Winkins. It has razor sharp teeth, which are essential for biting into and killing the innocent, even if they are not its primary weapon. But the real reason why Valak is so terrifying is because it mostly pretends to be a nun. Nuns are supposed to help innocent Christians, so a nun betraying God and choosing to kill is always horrifying, because it makes you too scared to trust anyone, even if they work for The Lord.

    year: 2017
    posterUrl: 'https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/9E2y5Q7WlCVNEhP5GiVTjhEhx1o.jpg'

      #3: Pennywise: IT (2017-2019)
      Pennywise is the definition of fear. He is a horrifying cosmic entity that feeds on the nightmares of others, and eventually uses them to kill the children and become even more powerful. One thing that makes him scary is not just the creepy Bill Skarsgård performance, or his meticulous design, but how he is your fear. He knows what everyone is afraid of, and does a seamless job at acting it out. If he made believe of my younger brother dying of inflation rising, I would be horrified. And the worst part? Every smart person has a fear. And overcoming it is complicated and very difficult. As a demon that is other people’s fear, he could not be away from this list. And yet, there are two more I find even scarier than him, though it was close.
  - title: Saw
    year: 2004
    posterUrl: 'https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/rLNSOudrayDBo1uqXjrhxcjODIC.jpg'
    description: |-
      #2: Jigsaw: Saw (2004-)
      Jigsaw was complicated to place on this list. Because, yes, he mostly targets people guilty of a wrongdoing, but that is the thing: everyone does things they likely should not do. Especially me. So he could very well come after me. But if he comes after you, you will be put in numerous escape rooms. But even if you escape, you will do so with broken limbs. This means that even the survivors suffer greatly. I mean, for one room, a contestant had to rip through another’s dead body just to get a key and escape. You never even see him, but the dread that he might be there is always dominant over you. And if you cannot escape the rooms in time? You die. That means that he is one of the horror villains where there is no way for you to escape Scot-free. Not bad at all for a man suffering from cancer. And yet, there is one villain even scarier than the likes of an absolute nightmare.
  - title: A Nightmare on Elm Street
    year: 1984
    posterUrl: 'https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w500/wGTpGGRMZmyFCcrY2YoxVTIBlli.jpg'
    description: |-
      #1: Freddy Krueger: A Nightmare On Elm Street (1984)
      SPOILERS
      This was a very close list, but Freddy Krueger is easily the horror villain I get the most terrified of just by thinking about him. He was a human who bragged about killing innocent children, so he was executed. Now, he haunts people in their dreams and kills them. Yes, this happens in their dreams, but even when they wake up, they do so in the afterlife. Yes, Nancy defeated him in the end, but only because she was a genius, who lured him into the real world. But she got lucky enough to be one of the smartest characters ever, or this would fail! Even after his death, he still came right back from the dead for the sequels. But what makes him the scariest is that there is no way to survive him. We must sleep to live, and when you do, he has a chance of being there! But that is just my list. What is yours?

Happy Halloween, friends! We enter one of the greatest holidays ever, at least in my opinion. Halloween is one of them because it has an interesting backstory, clear evolution and plenty of fun things to do? But what does a movie critic usually do for Halloween? While not every horror movie is released in October, many are. They do not all have villains, but plenty of them do. We can enjoy being creeped out by these antagonists all while finding them interesting. But at the end of the day, which are the scariest of them all? 10 villains will be on this list and 10 of them are the most terrifying. A gruesome kill is not enough. Today, I will look at the ones with the most kills, the difficulty of surviving them, and why we must be scared of them. And a side note, some of these movies I did not watch and found these villains out another way. Finally, some terrifying villains like Michael Myers and Hannibal Lecter barely missed the cut, but are honorable mentions. I am MovieMan, and this is “The Top 10 Scariest Villains Of The Horror Genre”
