Greetings. I am setting out to review all of the MCU movies before Avengers: Doomsday (2026). For the list, I started out with Iron Man (2008). The next film on the docket is **Thor: The Dark World (2013)**. This movie had a lot going on in the production room, with many directors trying to take on the role, writing creative differences and a real obsession with Loki. But none of it panned well. So here is my review of Thor 2: The Dark World (2013).\*\*
Story\*\*
=========

# The story is so poorly written and boring. While I can admire that the Marvel Cinematic Universe did try with the story, it had no potential whatsoever. First off, the heart is nonexistent. There is nothing that warrants even a teardrop from me. And the biggest example is when Kurse (Malekith’s henchman) kills Frigga. We literally just met her, so killing her off was not heartbreaking. It was just pointless. Second, the comedy is super unfunny. I never laughed, giggled or even chuckled. I just rolled my eyes. The movie thinks that the louder it is and the more jokes there are, the funnier it will be. That is not true here. Third: it is very tonally inconsistent. During some scenes, the film is incredibly somber. Others, it is very unhumorous. It has no idea what it wants its tone to be like. But the worst part is the the story is incredibly boring. The film begs you to pay attention to what happens, only it never has the right for us to even care. **VERDICT: 0/20**

# **Visuals**

# This movie is one of the ugliest-looking I have ever seen in my entire life. First off, every action scene is forgettable. I cannot even remember them, because they have little-to-no purpose in the story, and the choreography is terrible. It is just characters hitting each other with no rhythm. That is the exact way n how not to do action scenes. The cinematography also sucks very bad. The camera moves way too fast, and shaky cam is way too prominent here. The camera can never focus on anything and this results in shots looking uncanny, in a bad way. The character designs look unfinished. The attention to detail with these designs is a real fall-off from the first. I never thought that the designs were accurate to who these characters were anymore. But the worst aspect is the color palette. All we see is shades of grey with a tad bit of red. They symbolize nothing and just look plain ugly. **VERDICT: 0/20**

# **Characters**

# The characters suck so bad, and only one of them actually works. Thor is our main protagonist. Thor has no character arc whatsoever. He starts off as a grieving idiot and ends up as a grieving idiot. There is no change, nor does he help his younger brother change. He just changes because of what happens in the movie. There is no purpose for this to be Thor’s movie at all. Jane Foster is his love interest. Yeah, that checks out! Jane is just some damsel in distress. She has no character arc or noticeable character traits. She is merely a plot device. And so are Thor’s parents Odin and Frigga. The two of them just love Thor over Loki, and never feel like good parents. That is all that there is. The villain is Malekith, and oh my god, he is absolutely atrocious! He wants to use the aether’s power to save his dark elves species. That could have been a good motive, but the dark elves are literally bad people, who deserve to go extinct because all they do is kill the innocent. The only good character is Loki. He has an actual character arc of using his powers for good. And with the insane amount of sympathy we had for him in the last movies, it was appropriate. Also, he does not just develop in one scene, but spends the whole movie changing. **VERDICT: 2/20**

# **Acting**

# Chris Hemsworth is Thor. Hemsworth gives in no emotion into his character whatsoever, like how he did in the first movie. He just is an angry and sad character the whole way through. He speaks and walks like an angry person with not the mildest amount of character. Natalie Portman is Jane Foster. Portman is almost always amazing in movies, however, there are some roles where she just sucks. This is just one of them. All Portman does is yell at and hit people. The list ends there. In other terms, she barely has a personality. Christopher Eccleston is Malekith. Eccleston has nothing to offer into his character. In fact, he spoke up about him hating his character, but part of that was his fault, because he puts in no personality whatsoever. I know he has talent as an actor, but none of it shows here. Rene Russo is Frigga. Russo sounds sympathetic and nothing else. Yes, that is what her character is, but she could have also had love and wrath, other traits into her characters. Every other actor sucks in this movie. Whether they do not understand their characters, or speak and move stupidly. However, there are two actors who do a good job. Anthony Hopkins and Tom Hiddleston. The two have personality and actual complexity. Hopkins feels wise and Hiddleston is charming, yet complex. **VERDICT: 4/20**

# **Music**

# The music is decent. I would not call it good, but it almost is. First off, the volume is too loud for me. Seriously! Every time I watch this movie, I hardly hear the characters talk. It thinks that the louder the music is, the easier you can take the film seriously. That is not the case here. Second: the film has too many music cues. There are so much of them, that it gave me whiplash. A good score knows to exist, but should also know when to tone itself down. This does not. Third: It sounds very goofy at times, which does not support the tone at all. The film is very dark, and the score just does not care. However, it was almost good, so it does have its redeeming qualities. One: Thor’s main theme is even better than the first. Thor’s theme leans heavily into old-fashioned, epic cinematic heroism that perfectly matches the scope of a literal Norse god. It sounds like they take the character more seriously than they did in the first movie. Two: It is energy nonstop, and sometimes it works. It keeps you entertained with its sheer high-stakes energy. Three: Each character has a different motif: Loki’s is a mysterious soft-key lullaby transitioning into low, brooding strings, Frigga’s is soft the whole way through and the dark elves’ is low-brass and electronic. **VERDICT: 13/20**

# **In conclusion**, Thor: The Dark World (2013) is a completely horrible movie. It is by far my least favorite MCU movie, and I would recommend it to nobody. **Final Score: 19%**
