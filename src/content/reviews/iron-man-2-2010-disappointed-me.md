Greetings. I am setting out to review all of the MCU movies before Avengers: Doomsday (2026). For the list, I started out with Iron Man (2008). The second film on the docket is **Iron Man 2 (2010)**. Talk about a movie in a rush! The second Iron Man movie with Robert Downey Jr., this film took two years to complete. And the worst part is that by the time filming started, the script was not even complete, so the writers had to make last minute changes. And while Iron Man (2008) was more about being a self-contained story, the sequel wanted to get audiences and critics alive for The Avengers. But is this movie still good enough?**
Story**
=======

# Honestly, Iron Man 2’s story is one of the biggest letdowns in all of the MCU. This is because I am less upset about how bad the story was and how good it could have been. Tony’s story of realizing he is his father’s greatest creation and overcoming his sickness could have been executed well. That and when he realizes he is his father’s greatest creation, the scene is amazing. However, those are the only redeeming things about this movie, because everything else sucks. First, the comedy is painfully unfunny. This is because the jokes are just too mean spirited and targeting someone. Second, the heart is empty. I never got emotional for even one second, and there were many times I could have. Third, the movie is incredibly predictable and mean-spirited. And finally, there is too much happening. The subplots are the Ark Reactor Poisoning and Legacy, The Government, Rhodey and War Machine, Pepper Potts as The CEO and S.H.I.E.L.D. and the Avengers. Too much happens and it stops me from even caring. **VERDICT: 4/20**

# **Visuals**

# The visuals of the 3rd movie in the MCU today are… so disappointing. Even more so than when you study for a test perfectly, for two hours a day, and get back a D+. Now, the visuals are not all bad. For one, the color palette is nice. We get main themes of red and yellow, which demonstrate Tony’s flaws and the danger he is in. The danger being represented by yellow is a bold choice, but it works. Also, the action with Black Widow is pretty well-choreographed. And I guess that the superhero suits looked good. However, it is noticeable how this looks far worse than its predecessor. First off, the cinematography is rather weak. It never supports the overall scale and looks like they wanted to do the same thing they did with the original Iron Man. Justin Hammer’s drones also look pretty stupid, and are way too big to be called drones. But the worst part is that the CGI looks so horrendous, especially with Whiplash’s whips. I never felt that they aged the test of time. **VERDICT: 9/20**

# **Characters**

# All right. I know I have been quite hard on the movie, but everything else is good and above. The characters actually work here, but they could be better. The villains absolutely suck. Ivan “Whiplash” Vanko is the main villain. Honestly, he might be the worst of MCU’s first phase. There is no personality to him, and his only saving grace is that he is kind of a threat. Also, why was Justin Hammer even in the movie in the first place? He attributed nothing. But everything else works! Happy Hogan is Stark’s bodyguard, and his loyalty charms me a lot. He is very kind to him, and I love it, because it elevates the humanity of the film. Pepper Potts is Tony’s assistant, and she has a real charm I do not know how to describe. As well, she knows how to be important for the story. But what is new is that they added her dismay of Natasha, and for good reason. Tony gets so attracted by her that she feels betrayed. James “Rhodey” Rhodes is Iron Man’s main connection to the military and his kindness makes him charming, even if one-dimensional. Natasha “Black Widow” Romanoff is his connection to S.H.I.E.L.D., and she could be better. She is presented as this hot plot device, and has no real character backstory or traits. However, she is pretty charming. Lastly, Tony “Iron Man” Stark as a protagonist has not lost his touch. He has another character arc of realizing his illness is not the end of him. **VERDICT: 15/20**

# **Acting**

# The acting is great here and actually surprising, but it is not perfect. Mickey Rourke is Ivan “Whiplash” Vanko. Rourke gives next to no effort in his role, and underrates a lot. Seriously! The only time he has any personality is with his pet bird. But even then, it is too little, too late. Sam Rockwell on the other hand overacts a lot. Now, that does mean he puts in effort, but he puts in the wrong kind of effort in the scenes. He is too goofy during the serious roles and too serious in the goofy roles. Now, onto the entire theme park of positives. Gwyneth Paltrow is Pepper Potts. Paltrow has a lot of softness at the start, but when she gets attitude to Black Widow, it  feels like she can change characters very well. Jon Favreau portrays Happy Hogan. Favreau is so charming in this role, and sometimes, his clumsiness always gets a chuckle out of me. Don Cheadle is Rhodey. Cheadle has a lot of great chemistry with Downey Jr. and he is humorous, yet respectable in this role. Scarlett Johansson is Natasha “Black Widow” Romanoff. While she is presented as a bit too hot with a bit too little character depth, Johansson absolutely makes the most out of it. However, Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark is the best role. His tone in speaking, his body movements, his eye movement, everything is done so well with this character. **VERDICT: 17/20**

# **Music**

# The music is amazing and the only one of the five aspects to be that good to me. First off, the volume is seamless. It is loud enough for me to hear what is going on, so I could understand better the scenes. However, if the music becomes too loud, that stops you from being able to hear the dialogue. Fortunately, that is not what happens, as the music is also quiet enough for you to hear every single beat. Then, narratively, the music is flawless. Every beat works in tandem with the overall story and in terms of introducing the characters. Third, the music stays memorable long after the credits roll. My favorite tracks are "Ivan's Metamorphosis" and "Black Widow Kicks \*\*\*". However, I do have a nitpick, as a critic must acquire: it kind of sounds aimless and confused during the second act. **VERDICT: 19/20**

# **In conclusion**, Iron Man 2 (2010) is kind of decent. It is a clear disappointment compared to the first movie, but it has good elements. The story and visuals are less than satisfactory, but the characters, acting and music is well-done. **Final Score: 64%**
