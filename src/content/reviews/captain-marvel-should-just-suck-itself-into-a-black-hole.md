# Greetings. I am setting out to review all of the MCU movies before Avengers: Doomsday (2026). For the list, I started out with Iron Man (2008). The next film on the docket is **Captain Marvel (2019)**. This is the final MCU movie, before what we consider as the swan song of the Infinity Saga. But is it any good? **SPOILERS!**

# **Story**

Oh, my goodness, Captain Marvel’s writing absolutely sucks! Honestly, where to begin? First off, the story is merely just some finding out her identity, and the characters are left one-dimensional. There are so many missed opportunities, and for one is the war between the Skrulls, and the Kree. There is a war, and we know who is good and who is bad. Care to explain on that? How did the war start, I would like to know. The comedy is also terrible. It comes at Carol’s lack of an understanding on what Earth is like, and I never laughed. I just sat there and asked what I was doing here. I mean, for one, Nick Fury received an eye patch, because a flerkin that looks like a cat named Goose scratched him. As well, the non-linear storytelling. The film thinks it builds suspense for the film, but because we can never get attached to Vers, it just delays emotional engagement. And now that Captain Marvel already has superpowers, she faces no pain whatsoever. This also makes the film extremely predictable. But I guess there are some elements that work, right? Yes. The pacing. That is it. The film takes its time to establish the setting and characters, regardless of how poor it may do. Also, the post-credits scene is great for setting up the next movie. **VERDICT: 6/20
Visuals**
=========

# Honestly, the visuals are just mediocre. Not good, not bad, mediocre. Now, that means there are some aspects I actually like, such as the de-aging, Danvers’ superpowers, and the production design. So let us talk about them: starting with the de-aging. It was used on Samuel L. Jackson and Clark Gregg, to make them look younger, and it is very convincing. They actually look like how they did in their prime, and they caught every single detail perfectly. Whenever Captain Marvel uses her protons, it actually looks like a sight to behold. They look aqua green, which reflects very well, onto her suit, and is the same color of the Supreme Intelligence, so it looks natural. And third, the production design. It really looks like it was made in 1995, with the textures and costuming. But what about the color palette? Pretty bad. It looks incredibly ugly, and mostly on earth, just shades of tan and brown and grey. I feel like the filmmakers never fully utilized what they could do. And that is as well the case with the editing. It is so choppy, that it makes the choreography hard to follow. And lastly, the final battle is just a bunch of CGI. It does look good, but I feel they could have done more with it. **VERDICT: 10/20**

# **Characters**

# Man, I forgot how horrible the characters are! I mean, say what you will about Thor 2: The Dark World (2013), at least it has one good character. That is not the case with this one. Carol “Captain Marvel” Danvers/Vers. Sure some protagonists in the MCU could have been written better, but AT LEAST THEY HAD CHARACTER TRAITS! There is nothing to her. Captain Marvel never feels like a character, and once she learns the truth about the war, she becomes an entirely different character in one scene. Before Nick Fury was the director of S.H.I.E.L.D., he was an agent. And he absolutely sucked! Fury is just lame. He feels exactly like every side character to extra-terrestrial life beings are. There is nothing difference, other than that he is an agent. And I could honestly say the exact same thing as I did with Fury. She is some character. We have no exploration on who she is. Talos is the stereotypical adversary-turned-ally. We explore nothing about him, and he is extremely boring with no character traits. This stops you from possibly caring about him. And finally, Yon-Rogg is the villain. Does it even matter that he has a relationship with Danvers if it causes no tension? As well, he is extremely one-dimensional. I never cared for his crimes, and his defeat it too easy for Danvers! **VERDICT: 0/20**

# **Acting**

# Brie Larson is Captain Marvel. Look, unlike many people, I believe that Larson is indeed a good actress, I really do! It is just… she gave absolutely no personality into this role, whatsoever. She plays only some character, with no personality, so you know she is just playing someone. You never fully get the feeling that she is becoming her character. Samuel L. Jackson is Nick Fury. L. Jackson never fully gets into his role. He just seems like a mentor who has good enough chemistry with Larson. He never really feels like his own character. Ben Mendelsohn is Talos. Mendelsohn has this wisdomous and grandiose voice for this character, which fits like a glove. Jude Law is Yon-Rogg. Law has no charisma, no personality. He is so boring, and whenever he moves his eyes, it is just unsettling. Lashana Lynch is Maria Rambeau. I mean, Lynch has meaningful chemistry with Danvers. But I heavily wish that she had her own personality. **VERDICT: 6/20**

# **Music**

# Do you know how bad the music is? It is… actually good? I mean, the sound element is by no means perfect, it definitely has flaws, the music tracks can be a little too literal, such as “Just a girl”. I get the puns, but they honestly cheated with them. And the music can be a bit too loud at times, though that is quite rare. Now, as for why it is the only aspect of the film that actually works. The main theme is excellent. It perfectly captures Captain Marvel as a character, even if it rips off of Star Wars a little. And I love the film’s tracks like the eight-minute battle suite "More Problems" and "I'm All Fired Up" for their energetic orchestral composure. And the tracks, while a bit too basic, are still absolute jams, that I love listening to, over again. And lastly, So, what did we learn today, friends? The film’s score while flawed, is still good. **VERDICT: 14/20**

# **In conclusion**, Captain Marvel (2019) has some good to say about itself. Such as the music, the post-credits scene and some visuals. But this film absolutely sucks, and only if you are a die-hard MCU fan, I would think you should even give it a try. I am happy for you if you actually like it, but I do not. **Final Score: 36%**
