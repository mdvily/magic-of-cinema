# Greetings. I am setting out to review all of the MCU movies before Avengers: Doomsday (2026). For the list, I started out with Iron Man (2008). The next film on the docket is **Guardians Of The Galaxy Volume 2 (2017)**. This movie is a highly underrated MCU movie. I think it is because many people compare this movie to the first movie, and I find this as an unfair comparison. This movie is great on its own, and does not need these criticisms. **SPOILERS!**

# **Story**

# Honestly, the story is just average. Better than Spider-Man: Homecoming’s visuals, but it could be better. The message of family is back and still a giver. This brings the character arcs together and is a great bridge. For quill, he connects with those who are not his father, and for Gamora and Nebula, it is each other. Another thing is the heart. Now that these characters are stuck with each other, they start to bond together. And the stakes lastly are plenty high. Ego wants to connect everyone in the universe to his domain, and Peter trying to stop his own biological father from doing it is heartbreaking, and intense. However, the story is not great. The pacing moves too fast. There are so many scenes, such as when Yondu kills the Ravagers, that just fly by, without the time that they need. Also, the film is tonally inconsistent. The comedy is absolutely everywhere, and is a real hit or miss. Some jokes are funny and some are just lame. Third, there are two different subplots: Peter discovering what he truly is and Yondu, Rocket and Groot fighting the Ravagers. **VERDICT: 11/20**

# **Visuals**

# The film is absolutely beautiful. The color palette explodes across the entire rainbow. Not only does this look beautiful, but it matches the tone of the film so well. The CGI is also absolutely beautiful. Especially the yellow coated all over some characters. They look like aliens, which they are, and it is something creative I did not think of. Third, the De-Aging effects are absolutely radical. Why? Because Ego’s de-aging is so elaborately detailed, that I actually believed that he was young for his species’ status. Four: the attention to detail. Every detail, such as Rocket’s fur or Drax’s scars looks real, and very clear. I mean all I had to do was see a character’s skin, and I knew which one it was. And lastly, the action scenes are incredible! Especially the final battle. **VERDICT: 20/20**

# **Characters**

# The characters are very complex with great character development. Well, at least, most of the time. Peter “Star-Lord” Quill is our main protagonist. We all know that he is charming, but at the same time, he helps with the emotion of the movie. It comes from him growing as a character, realizing the connections he has with the other characters, not just his biological father. Gamora is a complex character as well, because she is conflicted if she wants to be a well-regarded hero or fix a broken bond with her sister. And the ending where she chooses both is so satisfying because it has been built up to. However, there would not be a character arc for the sisters that meaningful if Nebula was one-dimensional. Fortunately, she is not. She is conflicted if she wants to obey her father or fix a broken connection with her sister. Rocket and Groot are also hilarious, especially when Groot wants to press the button that will kill everyone. But their connection together also happens to have a lot of heart, because them being stuck together is something they both use to make it work. Ego the living planet is the villain this time around, and he is a very real improvement over Ronan. This is because he is more complex and actually has a relationship with Quill. This makes his eventual defeat heartbreaking. But what is even more so? Yondu’s gradual love for his boy. He slowly betrays Ego and becomes nicer to Peter. I just wish that Mantis and Drax had more to work with. They are funny, but that is kind of it, really. **VERDICT: 18/20**

# **Acting**

# Chris Pratt is Peter “Star-Lord” Quill. Pratt is well-known for playing high-energy characters like Emmet Brickowski and Andy Dwyer, which he does excellently here. However, there is also a noticeable amount of emotion in him, like when Quill begs Yondu not to give up his life. Zoe Saldaña is Gamora. Saldaña has even more range, though it is more gradual. She starts out as stern, like when she yells at Baby Groot for dancing in a dangerous zone, or when she grabs her sister away from Ayesha’s clutches. But she has a soft side, like when she explains to Baby Groot she will be gone for a couple of days. For Cooper’s performance, I want you to imagine Star-Lord’s portrayal by Pratt, and multiply it by two. And surprisingly, it works! It perfectly matches Rocket’s character and is just a mere joy to watch. Dave Bautista is Drax The Destroyer. Bautista for me is the funniest of the bunch. He has the funniest of one-liners, such as “You must be so embarrassed!” These are already humorous lines, but his voice amplifies it. Von Diesel is Baby Groot. Yes, “I am Groot” is all he says, but so what? The tone he says it in is always different from the last way it was said, so you still know what he meant. Pom Klementieff is Mantis. Klementieff is a mixture between Rooker and Bautista. From Rooker, she has the emotional anchor, and she gets her nicely done comedic factor from Bautista. Karen Gillan is Nebula. Gillan has an incredible, robotic voice for this movie, and her body move,Mets do not disappoint. Kurt Russell is Ego The Living Planet. Russell is a legendary actor and this is not the movie where he lets us down. Russell has a fear factor, sure, but he also has great chemistry with Quill. Michael Rooker is Yondu Udonta. Rooker has an amazing voice for this character and his emotional range is extreme. **VERDICT: 20/20**

# **Music**

# Many people call this the greatest score of the entire MCU. I respectfully disagree, even though there is a lot of good to say about it. I think we should definitely start with the good. For one, the soundtrack is absolute fire. “The Chain” by Fleetwood Mac is an absolute jam to listen to during the final battle, while sounding heartbreaking. The first track is “Mr. Blue Sky” by Electric Light Orchestra, which captures the theme of fun, which is what the guardians do best. “Brandy” is the heartbreaking licensed ballad between Meredith and Ego, and it is heartbreaking because it suggests that even though Ego killed Peter’s mother, he still loved her. This also means that the music, while catchy is here for a reason: to narrate the mood of the story. Another thing I like is that the music perfectly gets right where we are in the story. It is explosive and vibrant at the beginning and ends with bass, percussion and strings for Yondu’s funeral. My only real criticism is that the music can be too loud at times, though that is very rare. **VERDICT: 19/20**

# **In conclusion**, Guardians Of The Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017) is one of the MCU’s and James Gunn’s most underrated movies. I find it that it gets unfairly criticized, and I am sick of it. So if you have like the first one, I recommend the sequel. **Final Score: 88%**
